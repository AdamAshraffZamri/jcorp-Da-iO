# 2026-07-21 - JCorp Early Warning Radar: first end-to-end run

## What was done
- Loaded the `jcorp-early-warning-radar` skill and applied it to `ai-engine/sample_kpi_data.csv` (100 daily KPI rows, 2026-01-01 to 2026-04-10).
- Converted the CSV to JSON (`kpis_input.json`) for the radar engine.
- Ran `scripts/detect_anomalies.py` from the skill - produced full per-row variance + risk scoring.
- Result: **5 Critical, 17 High, 78 Low**. The 5 Critical hits align exactly with the 5 outliers injected by `generate_sample_data.py` at indices [10, 25, 47, 63, 81] (dates 2026-01-11, 2026-01-26, 2026-02-17, 2026-03-05, 2026-03-23).
- Built a focused executive report (`anomaly_report.md`) and filtered alerts JSON (`anomalies_alerts.json`).

## Key finding
Three of the five Critical hits are NEGATIVE revenue figures (-230k, -288k, -140k) on different days - physically impossible for a healthy sales ledger. Strongest hypothesis: sign-flip / refund-batch / data-load error in the Finance pipeline. Two Operations hits are duplicate-count spikes (+264% and +411%). No same-day cross-domain correlation - the failures are independent.
